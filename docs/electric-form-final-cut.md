# Electric Form Phase 6: Final Cut

Final Cut makes Electric Form the default product language instead of a collection of highlighted surfaces. The phase deliberately changes presentation, not athlete truth.

## Product-wide convergence

The authenticated shell now defaults to the Electric variant. Secondary athlete surfaces inherit a dark semantic token layer for ink, muted text, surfaces, canvas, borders, primary actions, warnings, danger, and shadows. Today and Coach keep their explicit Electric variants, while Progress, Training, activity detail, dossiers, Settings, and archive recovery no longer fall back to the legacy light chrome.

Shared form controls and state surfaces use Electric Form semantic tokens rather than hard-coded white cards. This keeps loading, empty, stale, partial, error, and recovery states legible without changing what those states mean.

## Responsive and accessible finish

Final Cut preserves the existing skip link, landmarks, 44px interaction targets, horizontal mobile primary navigation, focus-visible treatment, and reduced-motion behavior. The compact authenticated header can wrap on narrow screens rather than forcing account, Coach, Sound, Settings, and sign-out controls into a clipped row.

Focus remains a visual contract. Sound is never required to understand state.

## Amanda and Sonic Body

Amanda remains a coach presence, not decoration. Listening and speech remain explicit athlete gestures, transcript/state feedback remains visible, and no model, prompt, memory, or backend semantics change.

Sonic Body remains silent by default. Preference hydration, page load, navigation completion, data arrival, and Coach response arrival never trigger playback. Mute and reduced-sensory mode suppress cues while preserving visible equivalents.

## Public legal surface

The Privacy Policy remains a standalone public route with unchanged policy meaning and no authenticated shell dependency. Its presentation now uses the same Electric Form dark canvas, electric accent, typography, and cinematic surface language as the rest of Fitness Pals.

## Truth and performance boundaries

Final Cut does not fabricate readiness, health, recovery, or performance state. Existing provenance and uncertainty semantics remain authoritative. Cross-training evidence remains first-class only where compatible with the metric being shown.

No new runtime dependency, remote font, heavy media, autoplay asset, backend API, database, authentication architecture, Garmin semantics, deployment topology, or myChat integration is introduced. Existing Electric Form performance budgets remain the release guardrail.
